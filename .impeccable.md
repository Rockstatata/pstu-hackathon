## Design Context

### Users

Bangladeshi mobile-money users operating one-handed on phones, often on unreliable connections,
plus hackathon judges viewing the system on a projector. The primary job is to move or account for
an exact amount and receive clear proof of what happened.

### Brand Personality

Exact. Unhurried. Accountable. Chorui behaves like a careful teller: it states what will happen,
protects consequential actions with deliberate confirmation, and leaves an understandable record.

### Aesthetic Direction

Premium fintech through restraint: light-first, purple identity, flat surfaces, generous spacing,
and the single approved gradient on the digital Balance card. Avoid crypto neon, gamification,
confetti, generic dashboard grids, and enterprise-grey banking. The Integrity experience stays dark
and projector-readable.

### Design Principles

1. Purple identifies the product and primary actions; semantic colours report outcomes only.
2. Amounts dominate the hierarchy, use tabular figures, and render through `AmountDisplay`.
3. Protective friction is intentional; ordinary navigation and reading stay quiet and direct.
4. State is expressed with words and icons as well as colour, with WCAG AA contrast and 44px targets.
5. Mobile layouts are designed at 390px first and adapt into wider, information-richer compositions.
